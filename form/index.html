About this form

This form builds an entry for the registry. Copy its JSON, then open the file on GitHub, paste it and propose the change as a pull request. The fields come from the schema. Before you submit, read the rules in the README: list a source for every fact in links (rel source with an accessed date), and never name a customer's domain. Leave out what you do not know. At the end of the form you consent to the submission terms; entries are published under CC0 1.0.
